2 · Root cause & the conversion leak

Why rankings are stuck

On-page/technical SEO is already strong (prerendering, schema, sitemap, llms.txt). The thing holding positions at ~38 is off-site authority — backlinks, brand mentions, domain age. A new domain takes 3–6 months of content + links to climb. Code can't shortcut this; it's the slow track.

Why traffic isn't booking

The site offers exactly one action — a high-commitment cal.com discovery call — and nothing in between. A cold visitor who isn't ready to book has no reason to leave their email, so they leave. You're paying (in content effort) to attract people and then letting 99% walk.
